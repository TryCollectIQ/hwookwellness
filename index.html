How to use

Add one dropperful (about 1 mL) to a splash of water. Swish for 30 seconds, then spit. On the go, place the drops on your tongue, swish and spit. Do not swallow.

Ingredients

Water, Glycerin, Xylitol, Erythritol, Propylene Glycol, Flavor, Ethylhexylglycerin, Zinc Citrate, Camellia Sinensis Leaf Extract, Vaccinium Macrocarpon (Cranberry) Fruit Extract, Potassium Sorbate, Menthol, Eucalyptol, Thymol, Disodium Phosphate, Citric Acid.

Do I swallow the mouthwash?

No. Swish and spit, like any mouthwash.

Will 30 mL last a month?

Yes at one rinse a day. Twice a day, a bottle lasts about two weeks, which is what the three-pack is for.

Does it replace brushing?

No. It's a rinse for freshness and comfort between brushings.

Does it treat any condition?

No. HWOOK products are cosmetics. For tooth pain, bleeding gums or lasting bad breath, please see a dentist.
